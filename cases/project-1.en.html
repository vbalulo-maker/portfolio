Context 2/6

From a Referral Model to Our Own Product

The situation

  • Before the transformation, the bank's customers received acquiring services through a partner, UCS (formerly part of Global Payments Inc.), under a referral model.
  • A fragmented customer experience: two contracts and two support channels
  • The bank captured less margin from the acquiring business
Core problem: the bank was losing both margin and valuable customer data.

Digital-first strategy

  • One-stop experience One contract, one online account, and one support channel.
  • 100% online onboarding Merchants could connect to acquiring without visiting a bank branch.
  • Full product control The bank controlled pricing, customer data, and the end-to-end customer experience.
Target: launch an MVP within four months and establish full control over the product.
Managing Change 3/6

How we overcame internal resistance

The key challenge was not technology. Traditional sales channels had little incentive to promote the new digital product.

What I did

  • Engaged sales leadership I met with heads of the sales channels and demonstrated how the new product could help them achieve their targets through cross-selling and customer base growth.
  • Aligned incentives I helped introduce incentives for digital onboarding, so managers received bonuses for customers connected through the new channel. This significantly changed their attitude toward the product.
  • Ran a branch pilot We launched the product in five branches, collected the first success stories, and used them to demonstrate the value of the new approach. Other branches then started adopting the product themselves.

What changed

  • Sales channels started actively promoting the product after the first successful cases.
  • We developed an additional acquisition channel through online banking, allowing merchants to onboard themselves.
  • The product became visible across digital channels, reducing our dependence on the branch network.
Product Discovery & Decisions 4/6

How we identified what merchants actually needed

Data & analytics
We analyzed support requests, transaction data, and customer churn to identify the main pain points in the existing experience.
CustDev
Interviews with small business owners revealed three recurring problems: having to deal with two separate service interfaces, a complex onboarding process, poor understanding of pricing and tariffs
«I need one app for my entire business, not separate systems for the bank and the payment processor»
Benchmarking
We studied leading practices from T-Bank, Tochka, and Alfa-Bank and adapted the relevant approaches to our business and technology environment.
Key product decision We replaced the traditional branch-based application process with a fully digital customer journey. The onboarding process was reduced to a few steps in the app.

Retrospective 6/6

What worked — and what could be improved

✅ Key successes

  • Digital onboarding from day one: the product was not dependent on branch sales and could acquire merchants through digital channels.
  • Lean resource management: We delivered the MVP without significantly expanding the team.
  • A growth platform for SMB banking: Merchant acquiring became an entry point for deeper relationships with small and medium-sized businesses.

📌 Lessons & next opportunities

  • Systematize customer feedback: the next step was to build a more structured process for collecting and using customer feedback.
  • Modern payment infrastructure matters: modern payment equipment can also strengthen the bank's value proposition and brand perception.
  • Automate merchant settlements: further automation of settlement and reconciliation processes would be the next step toward scaling the operation.
Project Completion 7/7

How the project ended

At the beginning of 2024, the shareholders decided to consolidate the banking assets. Business activity within my department was frozen, and the integration into T-Bank's structure began.

My role during the transition: I managed the customer migration process and transferred product knowledge to the integration team, ensuring a smooth transition for business customers. The project was completed according to the integration plan.
