Integrating QR Payments into Merchant Acquiring

In 2020, the Bank of Russia and the National Payment Card System (NSPK) launched a new payment instrument within the Faster Payments System (SBP): C2B payments, allowing individuals to pay businesses by scanning a QR code. The goal was to make digital payment acceptance more accessible for businesses and reduce transaction costs. My responsibility was to integrate this new payment method into MTS Bank's merchant acquiring product and scale it across the bank's partner network.

My role:

  • Led the launch of SBP C2B as a new payment method for merchant acquiring customers.
  • Integrating SBP into the existing payment infrastructure and product bundles.
  • Defining the product scaling strategy, expanding the partner ecosystem, including PSPs and marketplaces.
  • Owning the product P&L and unit economics, including LTV and CAC.

Making QR payments work across the POS network

The first major challenge was compatibility between SBP and the bank's existing POS terminals. The terminal estate included devices from different vendors and generations, which resulted in payment errors during the initial implementation.
