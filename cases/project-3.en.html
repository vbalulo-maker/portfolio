Scaling B2B acquiring through PSP partnerships

We tripled the B2B customer portfolio from 4,000 to 12,000 active customers by building a partner acquisition channel and integrating the bank with key payment service providers (PSPs).
4 000 → 12 000 Active B2B customers over 3.5 years
+300% Growth in merchant acquiring profitability
80% Of new merchant connections generated through partner channels
Context 2/6

From Direct Sales to Partner-Led Growth

The starting point

  • Portfolio: 4 000 business customers
  • Primary Acquisition channel was the bank's direct sales team
  • The model had limited reach and relatively high customer acquisition costs
The challenge We needed to scale the B2B customer base without simply expanding the direct sales organization.

Key Challenges

  • Technical integration: Integrating the bank's acquiring platform with external PSP systems required host-to-host connectivity, API specifications, and coordinated implementation.
  • Merchant onboarding: Partner-referred customers needed to be verified and onboarded quickly.
  • Customer quality: We needed to make sure that partner-sourced merchants generated sustainable value through LTV and cross-sell potential.
  • Operational scalability: Manual verification and onboarding created a potential bottleneck as the partner channel grew.
Partner Strategy 3/6

How we selected PSPs

Key criteria

  • Market position: We prioritized market leaders with a large merchant base.
  • Technical compatibility: The partner had to support host-to-host integration and provide the required technical capabilities.
  • Target customer overlap: The partner's customer base had to match our target SMB segment.
  • Integration readiness: Available APIs, documentation, and technical resources were critical for a fast integration.

Key partners

  • Evotor — Market leader in smart POS terminals.
  • Smartfin — Provider of SoftPOS and payment solutions.
  • PrimiKartu (INPAS) — Provider of payment equipment and payment solutions.
  • Litebox — Provider of online cash registers for SMBs.
Результат These partnerships became a major source of new merchant connections.
Actions 4/6

Turning Partnerships into a Scalable Channel

Standardizing technical integration

  • I involved the bank's processing team in developing joint API specifications with our partners.
  • we used a phased approach: MVP → testing → iteration → scale.
The Team Product Manager + Business Analyst + Support Manager

Merchant onboarding

  • Automating onboarding: We introduced API-based data exchange with PSPs, allowing merchant information to be transferred directly to the bank.
  • Merchant onboarding time was reduced from several days to several hours. This was critical for scaling the channel without increasing operational headcount at the same rate as customer acquisition.
Business Impact 5/6

Business Impact

4 000 → 12 000
The customer portfolio tripled over 3.5 years.
+300% acquiring profitability
The growth of the merchant base translated into significant growth in acquiring profitability.
Lower CAC
Customer acquisition through PSP partnerships was more cost-efficient than the bank's direct sales channel.

Business Impact

  • Higher LTV — Partner-sourced customers demonstrated above-average LTV, partly driven by cross-selling additional banking products.
  • Cross-sell opportunities — Merchants acquired through partner channels also adopted other bank products, including: business current accounts, payroll services, other transaction banking products
Why the Model Scaled: The key strategic shift was moving from linear growth through direct sales to a scalable partner-led acquisition model.
